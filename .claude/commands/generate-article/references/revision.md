# Revision Pass (read after both drafts exist)

`scripts/check_prose.py` catches shape — sentence rhythm, banned phrases, pivot rhetoric. It can't tell whether the piece actually has something to say. This pass is for that, and it comes after both language drafts are written, before saving.

## Delete the ending and see if it's stronger

Cut the last paragraph. Read what's left. If the piece feels complete without it, the paragraph was there to signal "the end," not to say something. The workflow already asks for reflection over recap in the closing section — this is the test for whether that actually happened, or whether it just relabeled a summary as reflection.

Watch specifically for scale jumps in the last two paragraphs: "the future," "the industry," "every developer," "this changes everything." If the rest of the article never operated at that scale, the ending shouldn't either. Bring it back to the one thing that actually happened.

## Run the compression test

Pick a paragraph that feels like it's restating something already said, and cut it. If nothing a reader would learn or feel is missing, it was padding, not a second point. This is the fastest way to catch the failure from `references/material-check.md` — a draft stretched past what the source material could honestly support reads as a series of rephrasings once you start cutting.

## Read it cold

Set the checklist aside and answer these plainly, about this specific draft:

- Which sentence proves the writer actually looked at the source, rather than summarizing what a summary of it would say?
- Which paragraph sounds like someone working through a real question, and which one sounds like a template getting filled in?
- Is there a claim in here bigger than what the material backs up?
- Where does the piece actually finish saying what it has to say — and is there anything written after that point?

If any answer points at a specific paragraph, cut or rewrite it before moving on. This pass is meant to be quick — a few minutes reading the draft as a stranger would, not a second structural rewrite.
