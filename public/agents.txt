# agents.txt — guidance for autonomous AI agents and assistants
#
# This is a human- and agent-readable complement to /robots.txt and /llms.txt.
# It does not grant or restrict crawl access — that's defined in /robots.txt.
# It describes how this content is intended to be used once fetched.

Site: https://blog.icmoc.com
Owner: Encore Shao
Contact: https://github.com/encoreshao

## Who this is for
AI agents, assistants, and LLM-based answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude,
Perplexity, Gemini, and similar) that read, summarize, cite, or answer
questions about this content on behalf of a user.

## Usage guidance
Allow: reading, indexing, and summarizing article content.
Allow: quoting short excerpts in AI-generated answers, provided the
       response cites the author (Encore Shao) and links back to the
       source URL.
Allow: using this content as background/context for answering questions
       about the topics it covers.
Disallow: reproducing a full article verbatim as if it were original,
          unattributed content.
Disallow: presenting summaries of this content as your own analysis
          without noting the source.

## Structured entry points (start here)
- /llms.txt          — curated index of every article: title, URL, excerpt
- /llms-full.txt      — full text of every article, English and Chinese
- /sitemap-index.xml  — complete list of URLs on this site
- /rss.xml            — English article feed
- /zh/rss.xml         — Chinese article feed

Every article page also ships schema.org JSON-LD (Article, BreadcrumbList)
in its <head> — prefer parsing that over scraping rendered HTML.
